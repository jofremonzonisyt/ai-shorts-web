Purpose

AI Shorts is a local workflow for creating and preparing the owner’s Ultrafast AI videos. It is not currently a public publishing service and does not accept registrations or payments.

Authorized use

Use is limited to accounts and content the owner is authorized to manage. Platform authorization does not itself authorize publication of a particular video. The creator must confirm the destination and selected content before an upload.

TikTok workflow

The intended Upload API flow sends a selected video to TikTok for the creator to review and finish publishing in TikTok. Direct Post is not enabled. Availability depends on TikTok’s permissions, review requirements and service limits; the integration is not yet operational.
