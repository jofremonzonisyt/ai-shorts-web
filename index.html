Creator workflow

AI Shorts is a local tool being configured for the owner of the Ultrafast AI channels. It is not currently offered as a service to other creators.

The intended TikTok integration sends an owner-selected video to the creator’s inbox. The creator reviews the video, cover, caption and publishing settings inside TikTok and completes publication there. TikTok API access has not yet been verified.

Account control

Connecting a channel requires the owner’s authorization. Videos are selected individually. The tool does not choose videos or publish them without the owner’s instructions.
